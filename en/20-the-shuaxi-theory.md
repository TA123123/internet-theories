# 🎮 The Shuaxi Theory (耍起理论)

> "Whatever it is — play first."
> — a life attitude grown out of Sichuan-Chongqing dialect

> Chinese: [耍起理论](../zh/20-耍起理论.md)

## One-liner

"Shuaxi" (耍起, Sichuan-Chongqing dialect for "get playing / get going") — an open-minded survival playbook folded into the "era of grand theories": play big when you have money, play small when you don't, play hard when you have nothing.

## Origin & hype

- **Etymology**: a Southwest-China dialect word (Sichuan, Chongqing) meaning "play" or "get going." Per MemeWiki, it spread via short-video platforms and, around 2024, evolved from dialect into an online life-attitude meme.
- **Viral moment**: in September 2026 it exploded in the Sichuan-dialect-meme wave; Zhihu ran hot threads like "the Sichuan-dialect word 'shuaxi' goes viral — netizens coin 'the blessed play on.'" Bilibili's Meme Guide systematically defined it as the core slogan of the "**Great Shuaxi Era**" and filed it alongside lean-muscle, big-water and big-sleep theories in the "grand theories" family.
- **Spiritual charter**: the sixteen-character principle of Bashan-Shushan storyteller Li Boqing — "**find money through play, play to spend the money; combine finding and playing, with play first**" — treated by netizens as Shuaxi's founding document.

## Core claims

1. **Play first, deal later**: failed the gaokao? Go to Chengdu and do rap — shuaxi! Lost your game? Switch games and keep playing! However bad your situation, keep the capacity to play.
2. **Finding serves playing**: work is the means, play is the purpose — don't invert the order.
3. **Play big with money, play small without, play hard with nothing**: unbind "playing" from budget; it's a mindset, not a price tag.
4. **Refuse internal friction**: shuaxi is not slacking — it's staying loose while trying; replace self-attack with self-mockery.

## Common usage

| Situation | Example |
|:---|:---|
| After a setback | "Project died? Weekend fishing trip — shuaxi!" |
| Urging a friend to relax | "Stop grinding — net bar / park / mahjong after work, shuaxi!" |
| Life-philosophy summary | "Life is just the word 'shuaxi.'" |
| Mutual encouragement | "The blessed play on." |

## How to practice

- **Schedule the play**: reserve a weekly block with zero KPIs — put it in the calendar at the same priority as meetings.
- **Low-cost play**: parks, riversides, Bilibili, board games, city walks — "playing small" is about state, not budget.
- **Set "hard play" boundaries**: play supplements life, it doesn't replace responsibility — "shuaxi" before a deadline is procrastination; after a deadline it's recovery.
- **Watch out for performed looseness**: when you're genuinely playing, you usually don't have time to post about it.

## Caveats

- **Don't tie shuaxi to consumerism**: Zhihu threads stress that "those who truly get shuaxi spend money where it cuts" — Sichuan-Chongqing "play" is famously low-cost, high-experience.
- **Dialect memes are regional**: outside the Sichuan-Chongqing context, "耍" loses semantic density — imitators often acquire the accent but not the attitude.
- **"Play hard with nothing" is a joke, not financial advice**: save what needs saving, pay what needs paying.
