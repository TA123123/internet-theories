# The Physical Gold Theory (实体黄金理论)

> Hold physical gold. Treat asset security seriously. Hedge against uncertainty.
> Chinese: [实体黄金理论](../zh/07-实体黄金理论.md)

## One-liner

When currencies, credit and promises all prove unreliable, anchor yourself with a heavy piece of metal.

## Core logic

- **Zero credit risk**: gold depends on no institution to honor it — the classic hedge against the fiat credit system.
- **Safe-haven asset**: capital flows into gold during geopolitical conflict, sticky inflation and market turmoil — global gold ETFs took a record $31 billion inflow in Q3 2025 alone.
- **Inflation hedge**: over the past two decades gold returned ~10.2% annualized, beating most fixed-income assets (though trailing US equities).

## Physical forms and choices

| Form | Characteristics | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bars / ingots | Closest to spot price, low premium; storage and insurance costs | Long-term ballast |
| Coins | Standardized, slightly more liquid | Collecting plus holding |
| Jewelry | High crafting premium, big buyback discounts | Wearing, not a top investment pick |
| Paper gold / ETFs | Liquid, no storage cost — but legally a claim, not "physical" | Convenience seekers |

## How to practice

- Clarify the purpose: capital preservation, not a get-rich vehicle.
- Moderate sizing: household allocations of ~5–10% of total assets are commonly cited; your mileage may vary.
- Regulated channels: banks, exchange members, brand stores — beware "high-yield" gold scams.
- Custody plan: self-hold plus secure documents; consider professional storage for large amounts.

## Caveats

- Gold **yields nothing**: no dividends or interest; you only hold price volatility.
- Bid-ask spreads, storage costs and buyback restrictions erode returns.
- "Hedging uncertainty" ≠ "always rising": gold has cycles too, and buying at 2026's highs is not automatically a good entry.
- This document is information compilation, **not investment advice**.
