# 🍎 The Apple Theory (苹果理论)

> "Shop at Sam's Club, drive a Tesla, use an iPhone."
> — Huduchen, 2025's most viral class-judgment catchphrase

> Chinese: [苹果理论](../zh/22-苹果理论.md)

## One-liner

Huduchen's "Apple-Android relativity": which phone you use decides whether you're an "Apple person" or an "Android person" — the most widely spread class-label meme of 2025–2026, and a specimen of speech that got the creator banned on CCTV's say-so.

## Origin & hype

- **The creator, Huduchen**: from Zhenjiang, Jiangsu; vocational-school educated; worked as an auto repairman before becoming a full-time content creator. Early content: handing strangers cash on the street to test what 100 yuan buys.
- **In H1 2025** he first proposed the "Apple and Android theory": sorting people into hierarchies by phone brand — Apple users are "premium, above," Android users are "inferior, below."
- **Peak in August–September 2025**: livestream clips flooded the internet, amplified by the iPhone 17 launch — "watch two or three videos and you'd hit Huduchen." Posting your iPhone or Sam's Club haul with his voiceover became a trendy check-in ritual.
- **Banned**: CCTV later disclosed details of his account ban, characterizing the content as "maliciously provoking extreme group antagonism and promoting negative sentiments of violent grievance" — a decisive footnote distinguishing it from ordinary entertainment memes.

## Core claims (everything can be Apple/Android)

| Dimension | 🍎 Apple | 🤖 Android |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Phone | Apple | Android |
| Supermarket | Sam's Club | the wet market downstairs |
| Car | Porsche, BBA, Tesla | Chinese EVs |
| Degree | 985/211 | everything else |
| City | Beijing/Shanghai/Guangzhou/Shenzhen | tier-2/3 cities, counties |
| Housing/family | by money and price | by money and price |

- The logic is too crude to withstand scrutiny — but in memetics, **the simpler it is, the faster it spreads**.
- The full set included "Apple housing, Apple car, Apple degree, Apple city, Apple family."

## Business model breakdown (how it earned hundreds of thousands)

1. **Manufacture antagonism**: extreme speech triggers team-picking; BBA/Apple/Sam's users get an "elite" rush.
2. **Angry-call screening**: opponents call in to argue; he chains "what's your degree?" → "Android degree, serves you right," converting outrage into traffic.
3. **Precision harvesting**: only ~1M+ fans, but "extremely high quality" — believers tip nonstop.
4. **Revenue figures**: pre-tax income of 113,000 yuan in May 2025 (Bilibili alone); 1,999-yuan custom greeting videos on Taobao at ~400,000/month; hundreds of thousands monthly "with ease."
5. **The reversal**: he himself came from an "Android city, Android family, Android degree" working as a Zhenjiang auto mechanic — netizens fired back: aren't you exactly the "Android person" of your own rhetoric?

## Caveats

- **Labeling = identity trial**: a phone is a consumption habit; his rhetoric turned it into a status marker. Critics called it "manufacturing sharp conflict topics, inflaming group sentiment, selling anxiety — all for monetization."
- **Consumerist trap**: followers buying the latest iPhone or a Sam's membership to "prove themselves" fall into the manufactured anxiety — you can't buy class, only the creator's tips.
- **It has merged with Fengge's slang**: "ligong is a repressed Android user" — "Android" evolved from a phone label into a general self-deprecating prefix for struggling men.
- CCTV's ban characterization shows this content **crossed both platform and regulatory red lines** — it's included here as a specimen of 2025–2026 internet phenomenon, not as an endorsement.
