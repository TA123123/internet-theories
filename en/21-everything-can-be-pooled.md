# 🤝 Everything Can Be Pooled (万物皆可拼)

> "Everything can be pooled: why young people are turning 'pooling' into a lifestyle."
> — trending Weibo topic, September 2026

> Chinese: [万物皆可拼](../zh/21-万物皆可拼.md)

## One-liner

From pooled meal delivery to pooled housing, pooled travel and pooled pet baths — young people have turned "pooling" from a money-saving trick into a lifestyle.

## Origin & hype

- In early September 2026, "#young people can pool everything#" fermented on Weibo and other platforms, with media following up on category lists: pooled meals, pooled milk tea, pooled housing, pooled rides, pooled travel — even pooled pet baths.
- Data point: per public reports, meal-pooling alone saw Meituan's Pinhaofan user base grow **over 40% year-on-year in Q2 2026** — the reach of "pooling" has spread from takeout into nearly every corner of offline consumption.
- It isn't an isolated hotspot but part of the value-logic wave: booming value e-commerce, rapid expansion of discount supermarkets, and minimalist saving lifestyles were summarized by media as "extensions of the same logic."
- In the grand-theories context, it often appears next to the Big Water Theory — Zhihu's portrait of young minimalist wellness: "threadbare Pinhaofan + big water + briefly chaotic sleep."

## Core claims

1. **Save without downgrading**: pooling trades time for price and organization cost for consumption cost — the same thing, cheaper when pooled.
2. **Pooling is social**: teaming up is built in, turning "being careful with money" from something embarrassing into a shareable skill.
3. **Value ≠ low quality**: pooled meal services cut the premium between "eating enough" and "eating well," making cost-performance the default again.
4. **An antidote to consumerist PUA**: when "be kind to yourself" gets translated as "spend more," young people answer with "let's pool it."

## Pooling types and saving logic

| Type | Typical scenario | Saving logic |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Pooled meals | Group takeout orders, Pinhaofan | Volume + SKU reduction, cost spread |
| Pooled milk tea/coffee | Second-drink discounts, split delivery fees | Hit thresholds, split delivery |
| Pooled housing | Travel stays, hourly rooms | Straight 50/50 split |
| Pooled rides | Carpools, intercity pooling | Empty seats anyway |
| Pooled travel | Group tours, pooled local experiences | Wholesale price for retail service |
| Pooled services | Pooled pet baths, pooled cleaning | Shared slots, shared punch cards |

## How to practice (pitfall edition)

- **Prefer official channels**: use the platform's built-in pooling, not stranger WeChat transfers — pooling is a high-frequency scam scenario.
- **Count the total**: pooling saves the sticker price but spends time, communication and favors; discounts under ~10 yuan rarely justify organizing a group.
- **Stranger-pooling safety**: never pay upfront for pooled orders; for offline pooled services (housing, cleaning), watch personal and property safety.
- **Don't pool for pooling's sake**: pool what you need; don't consume *more* because pooling is available — that defeats the purpose (bridges to the Minimalist Consumption Theory).

## Caveats

- **Time cost**: the hidden cost of pooling is organizing and waiting — "saves money, costs energy" is a common complaint.
- **Food safety and service quality vary**: quality control of cheap pooled meals and after-sales responsibility for pooled services are real pain points.
- **Data caveat**: growth figures cited here come from media reports (Q2 2026 Pinhaofan users +40% YoY), not official financial disclosures.
- The mirror side of pooling culture is the debate over consumption downgrade and income expectations — it's a result, not a cause; fine as a personal finance habit, careful as a societal conclusion.
