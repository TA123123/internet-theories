# ₿ The Bitcoin DCA Theory (比特币定投理论)

> Dollar-cost average into Bitcoin over the long run, embrace digital assets, pursue financial freedom.
> Chinese: [比特币定投理论](../zh/06-比特币定投理论.md)

## One-liner

Never ask whether the market is up or down: invest a fixed amount on a fixed schedule, and let time average your cost basis.

## Core logic (Dollar-Cost Averaging)

- **Dollar-Cost Averaging**: fixed periods, fixed amounts — low prices buy more coin per yuan, high prices less, averaging down your cost over time.
- **Anti-human design**: removes "timing the market," the hardest decision of all, replacing it with discipline instead of emotion.
- **The long-horizon narrative**: belief in Bitcoin's scarcity (21 million cap) and the long-term value of digital assets — expressed through mechanical accumulation.

## Backtest illustration

- Third-party data analysis: monthly $100 DCA over the past 10 years produced a portfolio valuation of roughly $350,000 by 2024 — historically beating most attempts at active timing.
- But note: the sample window spans a mega bull run. **Past performance does not indicate future results.**

## How to practice

- **Amount discipline**: only long-term idle money; a fixed monthly amount you could lose entirely without pain.
- **Venues**: compliant, liquid exchanges; move long-term holdings into cold storage you control.
- **Automate**: set up automatic recurring buys to strip emotion out of execution.
- **Position sizing**: treat it as the high-risk sleeve of a portfolio (commonly suggested at a single-digit percentage of investable assets — varies by person).

## Risks & caveats

- Bitcoin is violently volatile, with multiple 50%+ drawdowns in its history; "going to zero" is a real risk.
- Consider regulatory risk, exchange/custody risk, and private-key loss.
- This document is information compilation, **not investment advice**. Never invest borrowed money. Never all-in.
