# The Athlete Physique Theory (运动员体魄理论)

> Muscular, but also with strength, endurance and athletic ability — healthier, more energetic.
> Chinese: [运动员体魄理论](../zh/14-运动员体魄理论.md)

## One-liner

Not just "looking trained" but "actually capable" — a full reserve of physical ability for life and sport.

## Concept

- **Physical fitness (体能)**: the integrated expression of organ-system function in movement, typically covering strength, speed, endurance, flexibility, coordination and agility.
- **Versus "lean muscle"**: lean muscle is about the aesthetic outcome of visible lines; the athlete physique is about measurable all-around ability — carry it, run far, recover fast.
- **Versus hypertrophy training**: bodybuilding-style training chases muscle volume; the athlete physique pursues balanced strength, endurance and athletic output.

## Core claims

1. **Function first**: muscle is a tool — strength, endurance, mobility and cardio are all necessary.
2. **Balanced development**: no lopsided training — chest without back, running without legs — lays down injury risk.
3. **Transferable results**: gains should show up in carrying luggage, catching the subway, hiking and pickup games — not only inside the gym.

## Training framework

| Quality | Example means | Frequency reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | Squats, deadlifts, bench, pull-ups, presses | 2–4×/week |
| Endurance / cardio | Jogging, cycling, jump rope, rowing | 2–3×/week, 20–40 min |
| Power / function | Kettlebell swings, medicine balls, sprints, change of direction | 1–2×/week |
| Mobility & recovery | Dynamic warm-ups, static stretching, foam rolling, sleep | After every session + daily |

- **Periodization**: wave intensity month by month — avoid running at one intensity year-round until boredom or injury.
- **Recovery is training**: sleep (the Big Sleep Theory), nutrition (the Anti-Inflammatory Diet Theory) and rest days determine what training buys you.

## Caveats

- Recreational athletes needn't imitate professional training volumes; "athlete physique" is a direction, not a prescription.
- Progressive overload is iron law: rushing weight is the number-one cause of injury.
- People with chronic conditions should get medical clearance before starting.
