# 🤖 The GPT Theory (GPT理论)

> Master ChatGPT and other AI tools to raise your learning, work and life efficiency — amplifying personal ability.
> Chinese: [GPT理论](../zh/04-GPT理论.md)

## One-liner

The gap is widening between those who can use AI and those who can't — turn the LLM from a "chat toy" into daily infrastructure.

## Origin & hype

- After ChatGPT launched in late 2022 and became one of the fastest-adopted applications in history, "AI productivity" moved from geek talk to a general skill.
- Within the "×× theory" family, the GPT Theory represents the tech-and-efficiency camp: unlike the "cut back" theories (sleep, water), it argues for augmenting personal capability with tools.

## Core claims

1. **Embed AI into workflows**: not opening a chat window when stuck, but writing, studying, retrieving, summarizing and creating with AI as a fixed step.
2. **Amplify individual ability**: one person + AI ≈ a small team; tasks that once needed collaboration become solo-doable.
3. **Keep iterating tool literacy**: new models, features and workflows keep landing — learning to use AI is itself a long-term skill.

## How to practice (study / work / life)

- **Information processing**: long articles, meeting notes, papers → have AI summarize, extract action items.
- **Writing copilot**: brainstorming, outlining, editing, translation.
- **Study tutor**: use AI as a Socratic tutor — quiz you on weak spots, explain, grade.
- **Code & automation**: assist with scripts, spreadsheet wrangling, workflow building (bridges to the Vibe Coding Theory).
- **Life decisions**: trip planning, meal design, price comparison and purchase advice.

## Caveats

- **Hallucinations**: AI fabricates facts with total confidence — verify anything load-bearing.
- **Privacy**: don't feed sensitive personal data or company secrets into tools.
- **Outsourcing thinking**: critical thinking and deep reading can't be delegated. AI is an amplifier, not a replacement brain.
