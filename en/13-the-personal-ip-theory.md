# 📣 The Personal IP Theory (个人IP理论)

> Build personal influence through short video and self-media — turn your interests into income.
> Chinese: [个人IP理论](../zh/13-个人IP理论.md)

## One-liner

In the era where everyone holds a microphone, run yourself like a company: positioning, content, trust, monetization.

## Origin & hype

- After short-video and livestream platforms became the traffic main stage, "personal IP" moved from MCN jargon to a mass topic — "the core lever for ordinary people to realize commercial value."
- Knowledge payment, e-commerce带货, brand partnerships and private-domain consulting all offer direct monetization channels for influential individuals.

## Methodology: from zero to one

1. **Positioning**: one vertical domain + one memorable hook (expertise, style, or personality). Chasing everything equals no positioning.
2. **Content**: keep solving your audience's real problems; treat the first ten pieces as practice — finish before perfecting.
3. **Persona**: distinct and consistent, so viewers can describe you in one sentence to others.
4. **Private domain**: traffic on public platforms is rented — sink relationships into a pool you own.
5. **Monetization loop**: knowledge products, commerce, consulting, brand deals — design the business model early, but don't cash out trust.

## Practitioner consensus

- Good content is the foundation of IP; growth tactics are only amplifiers.
- Building IP is a long-term project of sustained voice and influence, not a handful of videos.
- Understand the business: IP ultimately has to serve value you can actually deliver.
- Don't shy from monetization, but stay measured — trust compounds, and killing the goose for eggs zeroes it out.

## Caveats

- Survivorship bias is severe: the top cases circulate endlessly while the silent majority is ignored.
- Don't chase hot topics at the cost of your positioning; a persona too distant from reality eventually backfires.
- The flip side of "monetizing your interests" is "turning them into KPIs" — decide first whether you can sustain the output.
