# The "Sunxue" Philosophy (孙学 · Justin Sun)

> "The essence of Sunxue is winning."
> — Justin Sun's own definition

> Chinese: [孙学](../zh/17-孙学.md)

## One-liner

A philosophy of extreme pragmatic survival distilled from a decade of Justin Sun's public statements and practice: winning as the essence, attention as the asset, uncertainty as the ladder. It exploded across Chinese social media in early 2026 as "孙学" (Sunxue, literally "Sun studies").

## Origin & hype

- In January 2026, Sun's nearly decade-old book *This World Is Both Cruel and Kind* (《这世界既残酷也温柔》) suddenly surged onto WeChat Reading's trending charts; almost simultaneously, "Sunxue" became a hot discussion across domestic and international platforms.
- A viral line captures the shift: **"We used to laugh at his madness; now we study him frame by frame."**
- Sun personally weighed in: "The essence of Sunxue is winning. You will become a winner — wherever on Earth you are, whatever era you live in, whatever family you were born into."

## Core claims

### 1. Attention is value ("the alien's dimension-reduction attack")

- The logic is brutally simple: **wherever there is attention, there is value.** What you buy isn't an asset — it's a ticket to the global front page.
- 2019: a record **$4.56 million** for a charity lunch with Warren Buffett — forcing the crypto narrative onto the center stage of mainstream finance; the conflict itself became the content.
- 2024: roughly **$6.2 million** for Maurizio Cattelan's banana taped to a wall (*Comedian*) — eaten on camera, turning a high-art circle into a topic even street elders could discuss. The banana was gone; the symbol remained.
- "Dimension-reduction attack" comes from Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem*: change the competitive dimension and rewrite the rules, instead of grinding inside someone else's game.

### 2. "Do what is correct seen from the future"

- "Don't be bound by today's stock; keep your eyes on the increments. Do what is correct seen from the future."
- 2013: leveraged all-in on Bitcoin; heavy bets on Tesla when its market cap was under $4 billion, pushing his personal assets past ten million yuan.
- Always first to the narrative: founded the TRON chain (2017), acquired Steemit (2020), NFT-ized a Picasso (2021), took over Huobi/HTX (2022), pushed the HTX DAO "people's exchange" (2024), partnered with the Trump family on the USD1 stablecoin (2025). His tech may not be the best, but the market always knows he's doing the most frontier things.

### 3. Reject stability, embrace uncertainty

- "'Stability' is not only a false proposition — it often delivers devastating blows to those who grow up depending on it."
- "I would rather die free-foraging in the wilderness than survive in the comfort of a birdcage."
- As early as 2014 he advocated "no house, no car, no marriage before 30," pouring time, money and energy into self-improvement; he sees **uncertainty as the essential incubator of financial freedom**, and freedom as what lets you stop compromising your values for money.

### 4. Grit is the last moat

- Methods can be copied and narratives replicated — but the psychological structure of "never collapsing" in desperation is almost untransplantable.
- After founding TRON, regulatory shocks, industry crashes and years of public siege came; he never left the table: daily progress updates on social media (however brief), even late-night "first days at Huobi" diary entries.
- Ying Zeng, who calls herself the "chief disciple of Sunxue," said she never saw him lose emotional control for even one second — "an almost paranoid 'will to never surrender'" and the life force that can still explode from desperation.

## Caveats

- **Deeply polarizing.** Critics recast his path as a "methodology for making money at all costs" and "arbitrage life": harvesting rule-based windfalls, manufacturing hot events to lift token attention, marketing-driven rather than industry-driven. Crypto circles still quote Huobi co-founder Du Jun's line that Sun "never gives up any means of marketing himself" — and ask how many people his "games" have fleeced.
- **Controversies follow the narrative**: the Buffett lunch cancellation saga, lawsuits to recover large transfers from ex-partners, and early doubts about TRON's technical originality are inseparable from "Sunxue."
- **Survivorship bias**: every Sunxue move carries extreme risk plus resources most people can't replicate (Peking University, UPenn, the inaugural Lake Hu cohort, era windfalls). You can *study* the cognitive frame — betting on increments, pricing attention, staying at the table — without copying the gambling-style path itself.
- Balanced version in one line: **"You don't have to like him, but it's hard to deny he is someone who thinks seriously."**
